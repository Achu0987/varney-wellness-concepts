# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
delegated: Vite multi-page project, vanilla JavaScript modules, Three.js, GSAP + ScrollTrigger, plain CSS. Chosen for speed and static deploy (three concepts, one build). Next.js/React from the brief were optional; revisit for the production build. (Inferred from the brief; the user was not interviewed because of the same-day deadline.)

## Users
Primary: Socialsect's project coordinator, who will present three homepage concepts to Dr. Joseph Varney, MD (Florida, USA) to win a ~USD 5,000 custom website project. End audience of the eventual site: adults in Florida looking for wellness and aesthetic care, and hosts planning private group events.

## Product Purpose
Varney Wellness is a physician-led healthcare and wellness business in development. The deliverable now is three different homepage concepts (not a full site) so the client can choose a direction. Success: the doctor sees a capability far beyond a conventional medical template and picks a direction.

## Positioning
Distinctive feature discussed with the doctor: private Botox Party inquiries, where visitors plan an event (occasion, venue, guest count, date). Must be a prominent homepage section in every concept.

## Operating Context
Client is not yet confirmed; project is at pitch stage. Domain varneywellness.com (was parked). Long-term vision (not for now): EMR, CRM, patient portal, email marketing, more domains.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Homepage only per concept; no secondary pages, backend, integrations or real booking.
- Botox Party section is a visual/interactive demo; it is an event inquiry only and must not promise treatment eligibility. Treatment, assessment, consent, supervision and venue suitability are subject to the practice's approval.
- Services, address, doctor bio and photo are unconfirmed: use clearly labelled placeholders. Do not invent treatments, testimonials, results, address or a Google Business Profile.
- Content/service availability (including Botox and event formats) must be confirmed with the doctor before publication.
- Newsletter signup may appear as a demo only. No real data collection (no PHI).

## Brand Commitments
No logo or guidelines exist. Three proposed concept identities (not confirmed brand): 1) Future of Medicine (navy #080E20, violet #7557FF, ice #8BDFFF), 2) Private Wellness Club (ivory #F7F3EC, forest #173B35, gold #C5A77B), 3) The Human Experience (lavender #E9DFFF, purple #38206B, coral #FF806B). Hero copy in the brief is illustrative and needs approval.

## Evidence on Hand
None. No photographs, testimonials, credentials or service list. Must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles
1. Working prototype over screenshot: everything shown must really interact.
2. Three genuinely different experiences, not three recolours.
3. Medical honesty: no guarantees, no invented claims, clear placeholders.
4. Real text stays in HTML; 3D is enhancement, never the only carrier of content.
5. Premium but usable on mobile, with reduced-motion fallbacks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic HTML, visible focus, keyboard-operable controls, sufficient contrast, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, mobile-first usability.
